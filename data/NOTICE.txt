data/E2podaci.dat
=================

The 15 (Vf, E2) pairs in this file were digitised by the authors of the accompanying
manuscript from the published figures of:

    Tsai SW. Structural Behavior of Composite Materials.
    NASA CR-71. Washington, DC: National Aeronautics and Space Administration; 1964.

The source report is a US Government work and is in the public domain. The digitised
numbers themselves are facts and are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0.

If you use this dataset, please cite the original report (Reference 4 in the paper)
and this repository.

Columns: Vf (fibre volume fraction, -)   E2 (transverse modulus, GPa)
